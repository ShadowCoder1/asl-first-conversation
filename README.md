# Your first ASL conversation

Learn seven beginner ASL signs (HELLO, YES, NO, PLEASE, THANK-YOU, WATER, BYE), then use them in a short signed
chat. The phone's camera checks each sign live, in the browser; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Sign videos and training data: PopSign ASL v1.0 (Starner et al., 2023) and Kaggle's asl-signs, CC BY 4.0.
The demonstrations are real Deaf signers' PopSign clips of the seven signs (trimmed, scaled and re-encoded), and the
welcome screen shows three stills from them.
Source: the `signs` branch of physical-tutor.
